Materials

What you can use for your frame, from home and from school.

Not allowed: glass, tin cans, sharp metal lids, food that can rot, and anything damp or dirty.

Everything you can use, from home or from school, in one place.

Decoration techniques

Layering

Glue shapes on top of each other with small spacers to create depth.

Mosaic

Cover a surface with small pieces placed edge to edge.

Repeating a template

Make one accurate shape, then trace it again and again.

Rolling and coiling

Roll paper strips into spirals (quilling).

Painting and finishing

Base coat, colour, then detail.

Mixing materials

Combine card, fabric, buttons and caps for texture.
