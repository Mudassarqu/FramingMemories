Decorating videos

Short animated how-to videos for every decoration technique. Pick your frame, then watch the videos in order.

How to use this page. Find your frame idea in the table below. It lists the videos to watch in order. Pause the video and rewatch any step. The videos are animated illustrations, so your materials may look a little different.

Six technique videos

1 · Prepare and paint the base

Sand, tape, paint and mark guide lines. 63 s.

Use for: Every frame, before decorating.

You need: MDF or foam board base, acrylic paint (2 colours), masking tape, damp cloth.

Tools: Sanding block, paintbrush, pencil, ruler.

Stay safe: Wear a mask when sanding MDF. Cover the table. Wash brushes in the sink.

⬇ Download

2 · Layering with card

Wave and Sea: cut, colour and stack layers with spacers. 76 s.

Use for: Sunburst Rays, Wave & Sea, Date Palm Oasis, Dubai Skyline Layers, Falcon Wings, Dhow Boat, Rainbow Arch.

You need: Cereal-box card (or painted card), card scraps for spacers, shells or paper details.

Tools: Pencil, scissors, PVA glue, hot glue gun (adult only).

Stay safe: Cut away from your body. Hot glue is for adults only.

⬇ Download

3 · Mosaic with bottle caps

Sort, plan and glue recycled caps round the border. 59 s.

Use for: Bottle-Cap Mosaic, Button Garden, Heart Chain (collage).

You need: About 22 clean bottle caps, small beads or buttons, painted base.

Tools: PVA glue, hot glue gun (adult only), pencil.

Stay safe: Hot glue is for adults only. Check caps for sharp edges. Wash your hands afterwards.

⬇ Download

4 · Repeating a template

Honeycomb: make a template, trace, cut and glue about 70 cells. 78 s.

Use for: Geometric Lattice, Honeycomb, Puzzle Pieces, Film Strip, Heart Chain, Bottle-Flower Petals.

You need: Coloured card in 4 colours, painted dark base, scrap card for the template.

Tools: Compass, ruler, pencil, scissors, PVA glue.

Stay safe: Cut away from your body. Keep scissors closed when walking. Use a small dot of glue.

⬇ Download

5 · Rolling and coiling (quilling)

Cut strips, roll coils, shape and glue them. 74 s.

Use for: Paper Quilling Coils, Bottle-Flower Petals, Button Garden.

You need: Old magazines or coloured paper, toothpicks or skewers, painted base.

Tools: Ruler, scissors, PVA glue, pencil.

Stay safe: Skewers are sharp: roll away from you. Cut away from your body.

⬇ Download

6 · Finishing and assembly

Clear cover, photo, backing, easel leg and stand test. 72 s.

Use for: Every frame, after decorating.

You need: Clear plastic, a 5 x 7 inch photo, backing board, easel leg, strong tape or glue.

Tools: Scissors, ruler, masking tape, PVA glue.

Stay safe: Do not force the hinge. Keep fingers clear when taping. Your teacher applies any varnish.

⬇ Download
