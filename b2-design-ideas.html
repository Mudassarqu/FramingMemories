What makes a good set of ideas?

  • At least three ideas, drawn neatly on paper (or digitally).
  • They must differ: window shape, theme, materials, decoration technique or stand. Not only the colour.
  • Each sketch has labels that point to key features.
  • Each idea has a short description.
  • Each idea is feasible: you can get the materials, use the tools safely and finish in time.
Need inspiration? Open the Frame Gallery for 16 sketches, and the Materials list to see what you can use. Do not copy. Mix and change ideas to make your own.

A well-labelled idea

Labels to add:
  • Hexagon window (say the size)
  • Repeated hexagon cells (egg carton card)
  • Warm gold and orange palette
  • Easel leg at the back (card)
Description: A hexagon window surrounded by cells cut from a template. The cells are layered to give depth.
