Choosing with a decision matrix

A decision matrix gives every idea a score for every criterion. Add the scores. The highest total shows the strongest idea.

ScoreMeaning
3Meets the criterion very well
2Meets it a little
1Does not meet it

Justify your choice

Use the criteria in your reasons. Use these sentence starters:

I chose Idea __ because it scored the highest total (__ points).
It meets my criteria for __ and __.
Its key features are __, __ and __.
It is feasible because __.

Present the chosen design

  • A neat, labelled drawing of the front of the frame.
  • A clear list of key features.
  • You may combine parts of your ideas. Explain how.
Your chosen design should still pass your success criteria from B1.
